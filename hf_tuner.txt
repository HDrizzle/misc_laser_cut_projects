# HF Tuner project

## Part dimensions

Both capacitors have 6.3mm shafts, mounting points are in line with the shaft, mounting hole size: 2.9 mm

### 100 pF variable cap

Mounting points are in line with the shaft

Panel mount points to back: 40 mm
Mounting screw separation: 16.3 mm
Width: 24 mm
Shaft length after mounting points: 14 mm
Height: 31 mm
Shaft center above bottom: 19 mm


### 150 pF variable cap

Mount points front to back: 51.4 mm
Mounting screw separation: 16.3 mm
Width: 24 mm
Shaft length after mounting points: 22 mm
Height: 31 mm
Shaft center above bottom: 19 mm

### BNC connectors

Min hole size: 11.5 mm
Mounting hole spacing: 12.5 mm
Mounting hole size: 2.9 mm

### Switches

Mounting hole size: 6.3 mm
Spacing interval min: 15 mm

### Inductor (for center-tap)

Core diameter: 7.25 mm
Core len: 25.5 mm
